Help test Meow Ball Rush

A silly cat runner for Android

Roll up a giant ball of cats, smash walls and beat 12 wild bosses. I need testers before it can launch on Google Play. It takes about a minute. Thank you! 🐱

1 Join the tester group

Tap Join group on the page that opens. Use the same Google account as your Play Store.

Join tester group

2 Become a tester and install

Tap Become a tester, then Download it on Google Play.

Become a tester

3 Play, and keep it installed for 14 days

Google needs testers to stay in for 2 weeks before the game can go public. A few runs now and then helps a lot.

Open in Play Store
Something not working?
  • It's Android only for now (no iPhone, sorry!).
  • If step 2 says the app isn't available, wait a few minutes after joining the group and try again.
  • Signed in to more than one Google account? Make sure steps 1 and 2 use the same one as your Play Store.
